Your way through focustube

You set up once (Themes, then Sources, then Fetch) and then you live in the Feed. Premium isn’t needed for any of it.

The app opens on an empty Feed. Press t first.

Once per theme: the setup

1

Theme

t Themes view: what you want to learn about
  • nAdd a theme, e.g. “Tech conferences”
  • eGive it keywords (they rank the feed)
  • ↵Open its sources
2

Sources

s Sources view: the channels you trust on it
  • nAdd a channel
  • cAdd a creator (a person on several channels)
  • or let the app find them
  • mSuggestions, then n to search YouTube, ↵ to add
  • IImport your Google Takeout subscriptions into Suggestions
3

Fetch

Nothing appears until you do this
  • RFetch the new videos of every source
  • fGo to the Feed
4

Feed

Only your sources’ videos, no “next video”
  • jNext video, k previous
  • [Switch theme, ] the other way
  • vTo watch / saved / watched / all
  • ONewest or most relevant first

Every day: on each video in the Feed

Decide

  • aAnalyze: read the transcript, summarize it
  • iShow the summary and the learnings
  • xSkip it
  • lSave for later

Watch

  • ↵Watch (in the browser by default)
  • PIn the app’s player, esc closes it
  • YOptional: sign in to YouTube there, for Premium without ads
  • wMark watched

Keep & teach

  • eWrite your note
  • LKeep every learning in the note
  • BbFind the books, articles and people it quotes, then list them
  • +-More / less like this
  • ANAlways / never this channel
Your + - A N reorder the Feed. p shows what it learned, and you can remove any rule there.

Whenever you need it

⌘K then >

Search every command, theme, source and video. ? lists the keys of the view you’re in.

u or ⌘Z

Undo anything. Nothing asks for confirmation, so try things freely.

E and ⌘,

E exports your notes as Markdown files. ⌘, opens Settings, where every option lives.
